`Sidebar` lines its parts up on one right edge: no border of its own (the grey frame against the white page is the split), a scrollable section ends where the header and footer end, and a collapsible group row built on a `<button>` reads left-aligned. New `Sidebar.Item` `suffix` for a count or badge on the row's edge, and `Sidebar.Footer` (named export `SidebarFooter`) for the bottom area.
