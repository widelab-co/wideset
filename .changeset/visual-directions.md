Visual directions and starting points. `Sidebar` takes `surface="frame"` (no fill, no line) to sit on the grey app frame; the default stays Figma's white sidebar with its subtle line. A new directions pattern names the four surrounds a Wideset app can have (Flat, Frame, Inset, Canvas), and `wideset-build` now starts a new app by picking one and copying the closest whole screen from the docs' Starting points, each rebuilt 1:1 from the Wideset Core Figma file.
