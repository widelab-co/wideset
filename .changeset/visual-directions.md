Visual directions and starting points. `Sidebar` takes `surface="tinted"` (a grey tint, no line) and `surface="frame"` (no fill, no line, on the grey app frame); the default stays Figma's white sidebar with its subtle line. A new directions pattern names the four surrounds a Wideset app can have (Flat, Frame, Inset, Canvas), and `wideset-build` now starts a new app by picking one and copying the closest whole screen from the docs' Starting points, each rebuilt 1:1 from the Wideset Core Figma file: Kanban, Process, Finance dashboard, Gantt (a takeover over the app), AI chat, Communication, Calendar and Crypto, the screens before the app (Sign in, beside a picture of the product or in one card, and Sign up, with a new sign-in and sign-up pattern), plus the task side panel that opens over any of them.
