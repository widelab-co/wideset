The visual directions pattern and starting points. The pattern names the four surrounds a Wideset app can have (Inset, the default, Flat, Frame and Canvas), each one `<AppShell direction>`, and `wideset-build` now starts a new app by picking one and copying the closest whole screen from the docs' Starting points (`wideset.co/blocks`), with a new sign-in and sign-up pattern for the screens before the app. `wideset-craft` defaults to 32px controls, sizes an icon button by the controls beside it, ranks text by strong vs subtle, budgets status colour, draws a selected row like the active Sidebar item and makes Gantt-style rows whole-row targets.
