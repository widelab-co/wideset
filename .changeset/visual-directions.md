Starting points and the visual directions pattern. The pattern names the four surrounds a Wideset app can have (Inset, the default, Flat, Frame and Canvas), each one `<AppShell direction>`, and `wideset-build` now starts a new app by picking one and copying the closest whole screen from the docs' Starting points, each rebuilt 1:1 from the Wideset Core Figma file: Kanban, Process, Finance dashboard, Spreadsheet (a client table worked cell by cell, with a summary under each column), Gantt (a takeover over the app), AI chat, Communication, Calendar and Crypto, the screens before the app (Sign in, beside a picture of the product or in one card, and Sign up, with a new sign-in and sign-up pattern), plus the task side panel that opens over any of them.
