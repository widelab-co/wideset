New `NavigationBar`, the app's top bar: `NavigationBar.Start` for the menu button and logo, `NavigationBar.Nav` with `NavigationBar.Item` page links (`active`, `disabled`, `badge`, `asChild` for router links) or `Breadcrumbs` on a nested page, and `NavigationBar.End` with `NavigationBar.Actions` for search, a segmented control and icon actions. The line under it is `divider`, on by default. The skills learn when to use it over a `Sidebar`.
