Scrollbars match ScrollArea in AppShell.Main, ComboBox, FilterButton, Modal.Body, Select, Spreadsheet, Textarea and every block. Add `ws-scrollbar` to an element of your own that scrolls, or `ws-scrollbars` to `<html>` for the whole app. The bars keep the system's behaviour: where macOS hides scrollbars until you scroll, they stay hidden.
