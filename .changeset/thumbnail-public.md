`Thumbnail` is public: the small tile for a file kind, a project, an app's logo or a picture next to its name, with `Thumbnail.Icon` (`ThumbnailIcon`) and `Thumbnail.Image` (`ThumbnailImage`); the PDF kind now takes the red token pair instead of a hard-coded colour (so it follows dark mode) and the video and picture kinds the Remix glyphs closest to Figma, in `FileItem.Thumbnail` too.

The `wideset-build` skill and `AGENTS.wideset.md` list Thumbnail and say when to pick it over Avatar or `FileItem.Thumbnail`.
