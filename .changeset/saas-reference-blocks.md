Seven more starting points, the SaaS screens a new product needs first: Analytics and Sales overview (two takes on key figure cards, with a trend chart, sparklines and progress to target), Customers (tabs with counts, filters, selection and an action bar), Customer detail (facts, open deals and an activity timeline), Billing (a failed payment to retry, usage against limits, payment method and invoices), Settings (a section list, a save bar that appears on change, a danger zone) and Team (members, roles and invitations). They are composed from Wideset without a Figma screen yet.
